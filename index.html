The HisabMate Engine

1. Exact Paise Math (Zero Floating-Point Drift)

Computers often suffer from floating-point rounding errors (e.g. ₹100 / 3 = 33.3333333..., causing 1 paisa to vanish into thin air). HisabMate immediately multiplies all rupee inputs by 100 into integer paise.

baseShare = Math.floor(totalPaise / k);
remainder = totalPaise % k;
// First 'remainder' participants receive +1 paisa.
// Guaranteed: Sum of Shares === Total Spent!

2. Greedy Minimum Cash-Flow Algorithm

Rather than creating a confusing web of peer-to-peer transfers, HisabMate computes each person's exact Net balance (Net = Total Paid - Total Consumed). Then, it sorts Creditors and Debtors in descending order and greedily matches the largest debtor with the largest creditor. This mathematically guarantees the minimum possible transactions (at most N - 1 transfers for N people).

3. Natural Language Narrative Parser

Our client-side NLP engine analyzes casual sentences. It matches payers with verbs (paid, spent, bought, chipped in or paid by), extracts currency amounts via regex, detects exclusions (except Priyasi, without Rohan), and specific subsets (for Priyasi, between Anuj and Plaban).

4. Zero Cloud Tracking

Everything runs directly in your browser. No telemetry, no backend server, no cookies, no databases. All outings are persisted solely in your browser's localStorage.

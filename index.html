The HisabMate Engine

1. Exact Integer-Paise Math (Zero Drift)

Computers often suffer from floating-point rounding errors (e.g. ₹100 / 3 = 33.3333333..., causing 1 paisa to vanish into thin air). HisabMate immediately multiplies all rupee inputs by 100 into integer paise.

baseShare = Math.floor(totalPaise / k);
remainder = totalPaise % k;
// First 'remainder' participants receive +1 paisa.
// Guaranteed: Sum of Shares ≡ Total Spent!

2. Exact Subset-Partition DP Optimization

Rather than relying solely on heuristics, HisabMate solves debt settlement using bitmask dynamic programming over disjoint zero-sum subsets (for N ≤ 14). If there are k disjoint zero-sum subsets, the minimum possible transactions is proven to be exactly N - k. For large squads (N > 14), it seamlessly executes greedy cash-flow with a guarantee of at most N - 1 transfers.

3. Natural Language Narrative Parser

Our client-side NLP engine analyzes casual sentences. It disambiguates item quantities (e.g. 2 water bottles) from prices, extracts payers, detects exclusions (except Priyasi, without Rohan), and presents an explicit review stage before committing to the ledger.

4. Privacy & Zero Cloud Tracking

Everything runs directly in your browser. No telemetry, no backend server, no cookies, no databases. All outings are persisted solely in your browser's localStorage.
